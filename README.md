# Marker Studio

Marker Studio turns a colouring page into a **marker-by-number guide**, using the Ohuhu and Copic alcohol markers you
own. It finds the page's sections, gives each one a marker from your collection, and walks you through colouring it,
one marker at a time.

**Open the app:** <https://spicychipmunk.github.io/marker-studio/>

- **Made for the iPad** (Safari), and it works on phones and computers too.
- **Private and offline.** No account, no ads, no tracking: your markers, palettes, guides and photos stay on your
  device.
- **Ohuhu and Copic markers**, for now.
- **In beta.** Testers: please read the [tester guide](docs/TESTERS.md) first (it's also in the app, under
  **Help › Beta tester guide**).

Marker Studio isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Ohuhu or Copic.

## Getting started

1. **Add it to your Home Screen first.** On an iPad or iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then
   **Add to Home Screen**, and open Marker Studio from its new icon. The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, so set
   up your markers there rather than in a Safari tab. On Android or a computer, Chrome or Edge offer **Install app**;
   a normal tab works too.
2. **Add your markers.** Pick the sets you have from the list (Ohuhu Honolulu, Copic Ciao, Sketch and more), or add
   them one by one: photograph your caps, scan them, tick colours on a chart, or type or paste the codes. Not sure
   which set you have? Scan 3 caps and the app works it out.
3. **Make a guide.** Choose a photo of a line-art colouring page, a picture you've downloaded, or a PDF, or try the
   sample. The app finds the sections (the areas you'll colour) and picks a marker for each from the markers you own.
4. **Make it yours.** Tap a section to change its colour or pin it. **Pattern** sets how the colours spread (a
   Gradient that flows across the page, Random, Blend, colours from a photo, or your own picks), **Colours** sets the
   mood and how many markers, and **Shading** adds light and shadow. **Shuffle** tries another arrangement, and
   **Surprise** (✨) tries a whole new look; **Undo** brings back what you had.
5. **Colour along.** Tap **Colour along**: the markers are listed lightest first, and you tick off each section as you
   colour it. Focus mode zooms to one section at a time. Your progress saves itself, and when you're done, **Reveal &
   share** shows your page filling with colour. **Share & print** prints the guide and its key.

**Help › How it works** in the app shows the same in three quick steps.

## Photographing a page

- Lay the page flat on a table and smooth out any curl.
- Use even light, such as daylight near a window. Keep your shadow, your hand and shiny glare off the page.
- Hold your phone or iPad **right above the page** and get all of it in the frame. A bit of table around it is fine.
  Taken at an angle? The app finds the page and straightens it, but a steep angle loses detail at the far end.
- If the app isn't sure where the page is, drag its corners, then tap **Straighten**.
- Clear, dark, closed lines work best. Scans and screenshots work too, and a page that came as a PDF opens with
  **Open the PDF** (under Choose a photo, or **⋯ › Open a PDF** with a guide open).
- If some sections come out wrong, **← Edit sections** lets you merge, split or add them.

## Adding your markers

- **A set:** tick the sets you have. Lost a few of a set's markers? Untick them on the colour chart.
- **Photograph your caps:** photograph each tray from above, caps up, in good light (about half a tray a photo:
  closer reads more). The codes are read on your device, so your photos never leave it; anything unclear is asked
  about, with a picture of the cap. It needs iPadOS or iOS 16.4 or later, or a current browser, and a one-time
  download of about 22 MB.
- **Scan or type codes:** type codes one at a time, or paste a list from a receipt, an order email or a spreadsheet.
  On an iPad or iPhone you can also scan each cap with the keyboard's **Scan Text**.
- **Ohuhu's older codes** (from before Ohuhu renumbered its markers) are recognised when you scan or photograph caps.

## Your data

- Everything stays on your device: there's no account, and nothing you add is uploaded. The only things that leave it
  are what you choose to send with **Send feedback** (and, for testers, **Send results**).
- It works offline once it's opened.
- Each device, and the Home Screen app, keeps its own data. **Library › Back up** saves your markers, palettes and
  guides in one file, and **Library › Restore** loads it on any device. While it's in beta, back up once a week.
- **Help › Your data › Delete all my data** removes everything Marker Studio keeps on your device.

## What it covers, and its limits

- **Ohuhu:** 364 markers, among them every colour in the Honolulu sets (24 up to the complete 320) and in the 48
  Mid-tone, 104 Colors, 36 Skin Tones, 36 Gray Tones, 48 Pastel, 24 Portrait and 6 Skin Tones sets. **Copic:** all
  358 markers, by the codes Sketch, Ciao and Classic share. Other brands aren't supported yet.
- **Colours on screen are approximate:** screens and inks differ. **Markers › Print a swatch chart** makes a page you
  can colour in to compare.
- Very detailed pages, hatched or already-coloured pages, and faint or broken lines can give poor sections.
- Picking one section on the picture needs touch or a mouse; with a keyboard, use Colour along's list.
- It's tuned for the iPad, in portrait and landscape. Phones and computers work, but have had less testing.
