# Before the beta: v318.32 – v318.36 build plan (decided)

Owner: Ben. All decisions below were taken by Ben on 22–23 Oct 2026, after Fable round R32 (eight checkers; reports in
the review file). Branch `v31832` from v318.31 (702ef20). Built in five stages, each a numbered release: tested in
full before the next starts, zipped to Claude outputs. Ben pushes and device-tests when he likes; nothing waits on him.

Ground rules for every part:

- The smallest correct change, in the code's own style, with "(v318.3x)" comments that say what and why.
- Think about what else it touches: iPad portrait and landscape, phone 390 and 320, Larger text, Undo, older saved
  guides, backups and guide files, screen readers, Safari (WebKit) first.
- A regression test per change that fails before and passes after. Run targeted tests locally (Chromium and WebKit);
  GitHub runs the full suite.
- Saved data: only the additive changes named here, and every older form is still read.

Already in f240282 (from v318.31): the iPad findings A1–A6 (Focus Exit pill and hand-off card bounds; Home features
the newest guide, started or not; Edit sections' note when the photo isn't kept; seed grid rows in WebKit; Random's
Balance row; favicon); Sensitivity 5 for straightened photos (SENS_PHOTO adaptC 9); crumbClean (first version).

## Stage 1 — v318.32: the speck fix corrected, bug fixes, small UI wins

### 1A. Speck clean-up, corrected (decision 1; Fable P1)
Keep Sensitivity 5. crumbClean ships only if, after these corrections, every test drawing below keeps its sections
and it still removes the edge band on Ben's Specks photos; otherwise it is taken out and Sensitivity 5 ships alone.

- **P1-1** Away from the edge band, a crumb must touch **two or more** loose blobs (paper between grain dots). A hole
  inside one ring, flower or bead is a drawn shape and stays.
- **P1-2** The 3 × and 12 × limits are sized from the page's mm scale (`minPx(minPosAuto())` or speckClean's own
  scale), never the user's Min section size. Min stays a counting filter only.
- **P1-3** segQuality counts as specks only crumbs under `ref` (minPx(MIN_DEF)).
- **P1-4** No stale `crumbGone`. Keep it with the detection: in the Undo snapshot, or derived from comps via a
  `c.crumb` flag.
- **P1-5** Edge-band rule: the ink stays unless the holes are mostly sub-Min, with a cap on the ink erased. A one-sided
  lace strip or fine hatching near the edge keeps its ink.
- **P1-6** crumbClean runs as its own segRun step, so the page paints before it.
- **E1-7** speckClean and crumbClean share one blob labelling. The output must be identical.
- **Tests** (test/v31832-crumbs; drawings from Fable P1's exp/a.mjs and d.mjs):
  - dotted mandala of separate rings keeps every section;
  - border of separate rings and border of small flowers keep theirs;
  - a 5-bead chain keeps its holes; a margin caption keeps its letter holes;
  - a hole counted at a hand-set Min (MINPOS 100) is never removed;
  - clean page plus srcColour 0.2 plus edge band: no "coloured" warning;
  - a one-sided lace strip keeps its ink;
  - crumbGone after Undo equals the restored detection's;
  - a timing budget at 2400 px.
- **Gate** (main session, Ben's photos):
  - Specks IMG_0068–0071 in the app at the default: counts within 3 of Sensitivity 1, and the edge band gone.
  - Photo benchmark: specks no worse than Sensitivity 5 without crumbClean (226), matched no worse.
  - Guide benchmark: no lost match.
  - Colour benchmark quick gates: 0 failures.

## Stage 3 — v318.34: the five features

### C. Keep the source picture with each guide (decision 3)
- **What:** the source picture of every guide built from a picture (photo, scan, download, PDF page; not the
  sample), at the size the guide was built at (≤ 2400 px). JPEG q 0.9 for photos; PNG for line art when smaller.
- **Where:**
  - The picture goes in a record `src-<id>` in the guides' store: {u, w, h, ts, kind}, validated by srcRecOk with
    SRC_MAX.
  - Its settings go in the guide payload as `src`, saved with the sections: page shape (pgShape), pgHint, corners,
    flat, rot90, crop, tilt, enhance, adaptC, sensUser, f35, and faint cut where it matters.
  - So the settings and the sections can never disagree.
- **Writing:**
  - Written at the first save of a guide built from a picture, and when Adjust photo changes it.
  - A failed write never blocks the guide. It says once that the picture couldn't be kept, and the guide behaves as
    A3.
- **Lifecycle:**
  - Deleted with the guide (sfDeleteDesign, libPurge); back with Put back.
  - Copied by Save a copy and by "(section edits)" copies.
  - Not in .msguide files.
  - Reset progress keeps it.
  - All seven fin- touch points are mirrored (P2-6).
- **Backups:**
  - A tick box "Include guide photos (bigger backup)", off by default.
  - Finished-page photos stay in backups as now.
  - Restore takes src- records when present.
- **Open:**
  - Nothing is read until Edit sections' Adjust photo opens; it shows a loading state.
  - Then: decode, rebuild srcImg through the same straighten / turn / crop path, run processSrc / srcPrep (gray,
    adj), and show the controls with the stored values.
- **Changing it after colouring has started:** today's warning that progress clears, with Undo (okGeom / resegment).
- **Guides without a record** (older ones): A3's note.
- **Tests:**
  - reopen after another guide and a reload, then Adjust photo works for a photo and for a scan;
  - the progress dialog after a reopen;
  - section count within tolerance of a fresh build;
  - backup with and without photos;
  - storage-full when the src- put fails after the guide put;
  - Save a copy carries it; delete then Put back.

### D. Three new harmonies on evenly stepped ramps (decision 4; Fable P2-7/8, E1-4)
- **Harmonies:** **Light to shadow** (`shadow`), **Analogous + accent** (`accent`), **Greys + accent** (`neutral`),
  after Monochrome in Palette's row and in the guide's GEN_HARMS. Surprise never picks `neutral`.
- **Ramps:** chosen as a path (DP over L-sorted candidates, maximising the smallest ΔL step minus hue-jump and
  closeness penalties, random among near-best paths). Used by Monochrome, Analogous, Light to shadow and the greys of
  Greys + accent. Saved palettes are unchanged; record the colour benchmark's new digest.
- **Sizes:** 3–6 when fewer than about 72 markers are in play, 3–8 above.
- **Greys:** one definition, chroma < GREY_C (12).
- **Picker plumbing:**
  - slot.grey and minC;
  - a GEN_LEVELS row;
  - palOnScheme branches for shadow and accent;
  - the note "Some colours are greys" exempts `neutral`.
- **Greys + accent with fewer than 3 greys in play:**
  - The button is greyed with the reason "Needs 3 greys among your markers".
  - A guide whose greys drop below 3 falls back to Analogous + accent, with a note.
- **Tests:**
  - ramp step ratio ≤ 2.5;
  - each harmony n distinct within range on Honolulu 120 / 320 and Ciao 36;
  - neutral unavailable below 3 greys;
  - the Surprise list;
  - the notes never say "greys" for neutral.

## Stage 4 — v318.35: engine upgrades (decision 8; Fable E1). Each gated: ships only if no benchmark row is worse.

1. **Page finder:** when paper "fills" or nothing is sure, fit four lines to the strongest long gradient edges
   (non-maximum suppression, per-side voting, corners from intersections), then pgJudge. Gate: the photo bench and
   the guide bench photo rows.
2. **Part-coloured pages:**
   - Ink = dark and low-chroma on white-balanced RGB.
   - Sections already coloured get an offer: "N sections look coloured already: mark them done?"
   - The "coloured in already" warning is softened.
   - Gate: h17 rows.
3. **Hatching:**
   - Slivers between hatch lines are merged into one section (aspect > 4, minor axis < ~3 line widths, same
     orientation ±15°, groups ≥ 6, regular spacing).
   - Edit sections switch: "Treat hatching as one section", on by default.
   - Gate: the zentangle rows, and no loss elsewhere.
4. **Threaded OCR:**
   - Prototype first: service-worker COOP/COEP (credentialless where possible), numThreads > 1.
   - Ship only if ≥ 1.3× faster in Chromium and WebKit, and the tester form, PDF.js, offline use and first launch all
     still work.
5. **Copic lost-prefix re-read:** re-measured with the reader bench on Ben's cap photos. Ship only if wrong readings
   drop with no regression on Ohuhu, HELD or TUNE.
6. **Export:** the coloured reference page as JPEG (q 0.9); the colouring page stays lossless.
7. **Limited gradients:** a 2-opt pass on ramp order. Gate: the colour bench (0 gate failures, smoother steps).

1. **Version bump:**
   - service-worker CACHE;
   - app.html homever;
   - package.json and lock (×2);
   - help.js WHATS_NEW;
   - CHANGES.md section;
   - docs/DEVICE-TEST.md "Before you start" plus new checks;
   - TESTERS.md and DEVELOPING.md where affected.
2. **Tests:**
   - full unit run;
   - targeted e2e for every touched area in Chromium and WebKit;
   - benchmarks as relevant (guide, photo, colour quick gates, reader);
   - lint and prettier on touched files.
3. **Ship:**
   - commit (explicit paths, no zz files, no photos);
   - git archive zip to Claude outputs;
   - Ben pushes with GitHub Desktop, runs the release workflow (docs/RELEASING.md), and Pages publishes.
4. **After the last stage:** merge v31832 → main, a full DEVICE-TEST pass, then v319 = the beta.
