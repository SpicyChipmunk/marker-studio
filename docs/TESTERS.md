# Marker Studio beta: a guide for testers

Thank you for testing Marker Studio. It turns a colouring page into a marker-by-number guide, using the alcohol
markers you own, and walks you through colouring it.

**The app:** <https://spicychipmunk.github.io/marker-studio/>

This guide is also in the app, under **Help › Beta tester guide**, and works offline.

## Before you start

1. **On an iPad or iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first.** Open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then
   **Add to Home Screen**. Open Marker Studio from its new icon and set up your markers there.
   - The Home Screen app has its own storage. Anything you add in a Safari tab stays in that tab.
   - In a tab, Safari also deletes a site's data after about seven days without a visit. The Home Screen app isn't
     cleared like that.
   - Set up in a tab already? Back up there (**Library › Back up**), then open the Home Screen app and restore the file
     (**Library › Restore**).
2. **On Android or a computer**, Chrome or Edge offer **Install app**. A normal tab works too.
3. **Back up once a week:** **Library › Back up** saves one file with your markers, palettes and guides. This is a
   beta, and a backup brings everything back if something goes wrong. **Library › Restore** loads it on any device.

## What it covers

- **Ohuhu and Copic markers only.**
  - **Ohuhu:** 364 markers, among them every colour in the Honolulu sets (24 up to the complete 320) and in the
    48 Mid-tone, 104 Colors, 36 Skin Tones, 36 Gray Tones, 48 Pastel and 24 Portrait sets. Other Ohuhu markers with
    these codes work too, and Scan also knows Ohuhu's older codes.
  - **Copic:** all 358 markers, by the codes Sketch, Ciao and Classic share.
  - Other brands aren't supported yet.
- **Colours on screen are approximate.** Screens and inks differ. **Markers › Print a swatch chart** makes a page
  you can colour in to compare.
- **Your data stays on your device.** There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you add is uploaded.
  The only things that leave it are what you choose to send with **Send results** and **Send feedback**. It works
  offline. The camera is used only when you scan caps (**Markers › + Add markers › Scan or type codes**) or match a
  colour.

## Getting a picture in

- Clear, closed lines work best: a page downloaded from the internet, a scan, or a photo of a page.
- **A colouring page that came as a PDF:** open the PDF, take a screenshot of the page, and choose the screenshot.
  The app reads pictures, not PDFs.
- **Photographing a page:**
  - Lay it flat and smooth out any curl.
  - Use even light, such as daylight near a window. Keep your shadow, your hand and shiny glare off the page.
  - Get the whole page in the frame. A bit of table around it is fine.
  - Taken at an angle? The app finds the page and straightens it. If it isn't sure, drag the corners, then tap
    **Straighten**.
- If sections come out wrong, **← Edit sections** lets you merge, split or add them.

## Known limits

- **No account or sync.** Each device, and the Home Screen app, has its own data. Move it with Back up and Restore.
- **Some pages come out poorly:** very detailed pages, hatched or already-coloured pages, and faint or broken lines.
- **Picking one section on the picture needs touch or a mouse.** With a keyboard, use Colour along's list instead.
- **It's tuned for the iPad** (portrait and landscape). Phones and computers work, but have had less testing.

## What to try: Tester tasks

**Tester tasks** (at the foot of Home, and in **Help › Beta tester guide**) has six things to try, in your own time:
add your markers, make a guide, make the plan yours, colour along, come back to it, and share or print. Three more are
there if you have time, then four last questions.

- Answer each one **Easy**, **OK**, **Hard** or **Didn't try**, and add a note if you like. Your answers stay on your
  device until you send them, so it's fine to spread them over a few days.
- When you're ready, tap **Send results**. You can send again later: it's sent as an update.
- Ideas and "this was confusing" are as useful as bugs. Write them in the notes.

## How to report a problem

- **Send feedback** is at the foot of Home, in the guide's **⋯** menu, and in **Help › About**. Write what you did, what
  happened and what you expected, then tap **Send**. Your device, the app's version and any recent errors go with it.
- **To add a screenshot,** tap **Share it instead**: it opens a message with the same details, and you can attach the
  screenshot there.
- When something goes wrong, the message has **Copy details** beside it. **Help › About › Copy diagnostics** copies
  the same any time.
- A guide that misbehaves: **Share › Guide file** saves it as a file you can send with your report.

## Leaving the beta

**Help › Your data › Delete all my data** removes everything Marker Studio keeps on your device. Back up first if you
might want it again.
