Help
Photographing a page
  • Lay the page flat on a table. Smooth out any curl.
  • Use even light: daylight near a window, or a bright room. Keep your shadow and your hand off the page, and avoid shiny glare.
  • Hold the phone above the page and get all of it in the frame. A bit of table around it is fine.
  • Taken at an angle? The app finds the page and straightens it. If it isn’t sure, drag the corners, then tap Straighten.
  • Clear, dark lines work best. If sections come out wrong, ← Edit sections lets you merge, split or add them.
  • Scans and screenshots work too.
Glossary
Section
One area of the picture, closed in by lines, that you colour with one marker. Its marker code is printed on it.
Background / excluded
Parts that stay white. The background is the paper around the drawing. Excluded sections are ones you’ve switched off in the sections editor; tap one there to bring it back.
Colour pattern
How markers are spread over the page, in Pattern:
  • Gradient: colours flow across the page along a path (serpentine, vertical, diagonal or radial).
  • Random: markers scattered over the sections. Main colour gives one colour about 60% of the picture, a second 30% and an accent 10% (tap the bar to choose them); Mixed treats every marker alike, and No repeats gives each section its own.
  • Blend: tap to drop colour anchors; colours blend between them.
  • Photo: colours taken from a photo you lay over the page.
  • Manual: you pick every section’s marker.
Zone
A part of the picture with its own pattern and colours, like a flower against the background around it. In Pattern, tap ＋ Zone, then tap or drag across sections to add them. The chips at the top of Pattern and Colours choose which zone those tabs change; Main is the rest of the picture. With shading on, each zone can be shaded or left flat, with its own roundness, highlights and shadows (Shading, under its chips); the light is the whole picture’s.
Pin
Keeps a section’s marker when you shuffle or change the pattern. Tap a section, then Pin.
Blend companions
A lighter and a darker marker for each colour, listed in the printed key, for blending.
Shading
Adds light and shadow with lighter and darker markers you own. Drag the sun to move the light, or, with the Photo pattern, take the light from your photo (Light from).
Highlight / Base / Shadow (H/B/S)
The tones of a shaded section. B is its main marker. H is a lighter one on the side facing the light (or a warmer one, or the paper left white: Shading › Highlights), S a darker one on the far side (or a cooler or grey one laid over the base: Shading › Shadows). “2nd coat” means going over the base again.
Values
The button with three grey bars under the picture. It shows your guide in greys, so you can see where it’s light and dark without the colours. It only changes the view.
Focus mode
In Colour along. It zooms to one section at a time, colour by colour. Tap ✓ Done (or the section) to move on.
Reveal
In Share. Plays your page filling with colour, to share as a picture or a clip.
Keyboard and screen readers
  • Picking a single section on the picture needs touch or a mouse: Change colour, Pin, Paint and ticking off one section.
  • By keyboard, use Colour along’s list instead: open a marker, then Mark all done or Find next. Focus mode and everything else work by keyboard too.
  • In Markers, press Shift+F10 on a marker to open its details.
  • A zone’s sections can be chosen by keyboard: in the zone’s editor, Tab to Choose sections with the keys. The arrow keys then move a ring from section to section, and Space adds the ringed one to the zone or takes it out.
Your data
  • Your markers, palettes, guides and photos stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • There’s no account, no ads and no tracking. The app works offline.
  • On iPhone, Safari can clear a website’s data if you don’t open it for a while. Adding Marker Studio to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) keeps it safer.
  • Back up now and then: Library → Back up saves one file with everything. Restore brings it back on any phone.
  • A guide missing from your Library may still be stored on this device. Find lost guides looks for any and can add them back.

    How it works
    Step 1 of 3

    Photograph a colouring page

    Choose a photo of a line-art page, or try the sample.

    • Lay the page flat, in even light.
    • Get the whole page in the frame.
    • Taken at an angle? The app straightens it.
    Step 2 of 3

    Sections and colours

    The app finds the sections — the areas you’ll colour — and picks a marker for each, from the markers you own.

    • Tap a section to change its colour or pin it.
    • Try patterns and shading in the Pattern and Shading tabs.
    Step 3 of 3

    Colour along

    Tap Colour along and tick sections off as you colour them.

    • Focus mode zooms to one section at a time and walks you through.
    • Finished? Reveal & share shows your page filling with colour.
